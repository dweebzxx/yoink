# Changelog

## [Unreleased]
### Added
- A "Show in screenshots" setting in Settings, on by default. When it is turned off, squares and their hover preview and click-feedback overlays are left out of screenshots and screen recordings while staying visible on screen. Saved configurations made before this setting existed keep working and read as on.
### Changed
- The default square size is now 40 pt (was 70 pt). The smallest square size is now 30 pt (was 40 pt). The largest stays 160 pt. Sizes already saved are kept.
- A square's label can now be up to four characters (was two). A four-character label is shown on two rows of two characters.
